## Background
### Before
He is a young prince of [[Monolithios]]. Since he was far away from being the heir, he fought in the Great Wars between Celestials and Devils.
### Life Changing Event
During the wars, he fell in love with a lady of fiendish heritage called [[Estus]]. Once his family found out, [[Estus]] was killed, he was stripped of his wings, and banished from [[Monolithios]].
