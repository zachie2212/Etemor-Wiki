## Lair Ecology
They've got their **space** and no-one except silly adventurers and crazy mages bothers them. They'll ruin your day/life if you get too close, so **other animals just don't get close**. Thus a basilisk lair would be typified by a **sparseness** of "normal" fauna.
Their petrification gaze functions both as a strong defence and as a hunting aid. Beyond this, whether they still hunt small game and feed on carrion or they ambush large game (including humanoids) and consume their petrified forms at their leisure... is up to you.

## Quest
A mated basilisk pair have taken over this thieves' hideout, as they nesting a few babies who require petrified meat to survive. As such, they are highly territorial and defensive.

Since the party decided to infiltrate [[Lawson Manor]] first, this task has already been given to another mercenary group called [[Etemor/Organisations/The Wanderers|The Wanderers]]. They will not fight the party unnecessarily, however they won't allow them to steal the reward or get in their way.
[[The Wanderers]] were sent by a delegation of [[The Hand]] in [[Oldfield]], who had not communicated with the sect of the thieves' guild in [[Darnathmore]], hence why both parties were sent there.
 
## Dungeon Layout
### Room 1 - Entrance Door
A wooden sign above the door, in Thieves' Cant, says:
- *The keys are under the mat. Share today's bounty to enter, my friend*
The sign covers writing in the stone, in the language of [[Lizardfolk]], which says:
- *Those who enter, prostrate thyself before the wrath of [[Semuanya]]*
The thieves' protected their hideout with guards, who are now dead, however the massive stone door is also magically locked.
- The stone door has some old runes nearby which have been chipped and scratched
	- Investigation reveals them to be tarnished magical runes, probably placed there by the original temple inhabitants. 
	- They were removed by the thieves.
- There is a stone door mat in front of the temple, looking out of place.
	- Inspection reveals the mat is not from this area, and has also been enchanted.
	- If they look under the mat, there is nothing there. If they put something stolen under the mat and check it again the keys are there. If they put something which is not stolen, the curtains and the mat become animated and attack the party.

### Room 2 - Trapped Hallway
- They find a long hallway approaching an antechamber.
- The stone tiles in the floor appear much more uniform than those seen so far. 
- The room contains some triggered and disabled traps. There are bolts on the floor, and a lingering smell of noxious gas around some stained holes in the wall.
- [[The Wanderers]] triggered some traps, but rearmed and avoided some others.
	- All traps have a DC 14 disarm. 
	- Tripwire bolt trap: the beginning of the hallway has a tripwire which triggers bolts to be shot from the far end of the hallway. DC 14 disarm. Anybody exposed must succeed on a DC 13 CON save or take 2d8 poison damage (half on success) and be poisoned for 1 hour. 
	- Pressure plate spike trap: the middle of the hallway has one raised tile. When stepped on, the second half of the hallway is pierced by spikes from the walls. Anybody in the area must succeed on a DC 13 DEX save or take 2d10 piercing damage (half on success).

### Room 3 - Temple Antechamber
- The antechamber appears to be a place of rest, there are cards strewn across a table, some bottles of booze around, and a few places to rest.
- Here the party will meet [[The Wanderers]], who arrived first and triggered/disabled the traps here, and decided to take a short rest before continuing into the main temple room. They then heard the other group approaching and hid.

### Room 4 - Basilisk Nest
The main room of the abandoned Temple. It is an underground cavern with the ceiling open to the forest.
