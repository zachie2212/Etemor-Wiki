## Overview
The entire lair is pitch black apart from the Kitchen (1). Filled to the brim with traps. The smell of mildew and rotting meat occupy most rooms.

## Layout
1. Kitchen
	1. 1 [[Kobold]] Tiro (Minion) stands guard while two others are cooking food.
	2. When PCs enter, all three will run to the Well Room.
	3. The hallway between 1 and 2 has a tripwire trap (DC 12 Perception to notice) which the Kobolds are short enough to run through without triggering. When triggered, a platform of swords will be released from the ceiling - DC 12 DEX save, dealing **1d6 bludgeoning** damage on fail to anyone in the hallway.
2. Well Room
	1. The well drops down to the lower floor. One Kobold will cut a large alarm pot hanging off the side to alert their allies below of danger.
	2. One Kobold dashes ahead to get allies to flank the PCs.
	3. The well is covered in scratch marks where the kobolds have been climbing.
	4. There is a secret door to the south leading to the Pit Trap. DC 13 Perception to notice it.
3. Bed Chamber
	1. Filled with lots of small beds.
	2. There are some small footlockers next to some of the beds. These are all trapped, and when opened (1d3):
		1. Spiders crawl out: DC 12 CON save, dealing **1d4 poison** damage and giving the poison condition for 10 minutes.
		2. Small explosive: DC 14 DEX save, dealing **1d6 fire damage** to everyone within 5 feet.
		3. Tar trap: DC 12 STR save, or restrained.
4. Shrine
	1. This room contains a medium stone statue of a young dragon. The smell of alcohol (DC 13 Perception: and acetone) fills the room.
	2. The shrine is surrounded by treasure offerings.
		1. 336gp, 700sp, 1750cp
		2. 84gp gems
		3. 3 ale barrels
	3. One Kobold Tiro (Minion) is hidden inside a barrel waiting for the PCs to approach the treasure before setting it alight. When lit, the flames quickly engulf the centre of the room.
	4. The flames do **1d6 fire** damage every round a creature is on fire. A creature can put out the flames with their action.
	5. The Kobolds will try to flank and attack the PCs while they are in the flames.
5. Pit Trap
	1. A pit trap is hidden in this hallway. DC 13 Perception to notice it. The Kobolds know to jump over it.
	2. A creature who falls into the trap must make a DC 12 DEX save or take **1d6 bludgeoning** damage.
6. Egg Room
	1. Most of the Kobolds in the lair occupy this room, protecting their eggs.
	2. The eggs surround a campfire, which is covered by a box if the Kobolds hear the PCs coming.
	3. **10 Kobold Tiros, 1 Kobold Veles, 1 Kobold Adeptus** 
		1. The Veles and Adeptus command 5 Tiros each.
		2. The Kobolds will protect the eggs as their highest priority.
		3. The Kobolds carry treasure on their person. See [[PRIVATE/Magic Items|Magic Items]]:
			1. 109gp jewellery
			2. 1 Tiro: **Reszur**
			3. Adeptus: **Magic Missile Spell Scroll**
			4. Veles: **Periapt of Wound Closure**

## Questions
- A good Investigation check will reveal that the Kobolds have not lived here long. Where did they come from and why did they move?
- There are no magic item shops around here. Where did the Kobolds get the magic items? Did they get them from adventurers, or is there a dungeon or monster hoard nearby which they got them from?
