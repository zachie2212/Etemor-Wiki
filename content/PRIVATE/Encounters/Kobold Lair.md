## Layout
1. Kitchen
	1. 1 [[Kobold]] Minion stands guard while two others are cooking food.
	2. When PCs enter, all three will run to the Well Room.
	3. The hallway between 1 and 2 has a tripwire trap (DC 12 Perception to notice) which the Kobolds are short enough to run through without triggering. When triggered, a platform of swords will be released from the ceiling - DC 12 DEX save, dealing 1d6 bludgeoning damage on fail to anyone in the hallway.
2. Well Room
	1. The well drops down to the lower floor. One Kobold will cut a large alarm pot hanging off the side to alert their allies below of danger.
	2. One Kobold dashes ahead to get allies to flank the PCs.
	3. The well is covered in scratch marks where the kobolds have been climbing.
3. Bed Chamber
	1. Filled with lots of small beds.
	2. There are some small footlockers next to some of the beds. These are all trapped, and when opened (1d3):
		1. Spiders crawl out: DC 12 CON save, dealing 1d4 poison damage and giving the poison condition for 10 minutes.
		2. Small explosive: DC 14 DEX save, dealing 1d6 fire damage to everyone within 5 feet.
		3. Tar trap: DC 12 STR save, or restrained.
