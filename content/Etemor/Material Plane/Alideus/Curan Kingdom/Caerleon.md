## Overview
The largest and oldest "University town" in the [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/index|Curan Kingdom]]. Home to one of the oldest schools of magic in the whole of Etemor, [[The Arcanium]]. Many of the most powerful mages in [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/index|Alideus]] live and research here, including many who sit on the [[Isongau Council]].
