## Overview
Large fishing town in the East of the [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/index|Curan Kingdom]]. 

## Description
Busy due to its location along the [[Woodwick Brook]], which is used by fishing boats all the way to the Eastern sea.

## History
It is believed that Pre-[[Cataclysm]], the human city of [[Trefoul]] was found in this place. For some reason, during the [[Cataclysm]] the city was washed away by the [[Lake Trefoul]]. 
As such, archaeologists and wizard researchers often conduct digs and dives in the area, searching for artifacts of the long gone [[Ali'deum Empire]].
