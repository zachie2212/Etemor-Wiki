## Overview
The mountain [[General/Cities|city]] where many [[Goliaths]] originate. Found deep in the mountain range defining the border between the [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/index|Curan Kingdom]] and [[North Alideus]] on the continent of [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/index|Alideus]]. 

## Description
This city is filled with the ruins of a once great [[Nation|nation]]. The homes of the current denizens have been built between and upon the gigantic walls and pillars of the ruined giant homes. Stone pillars host many different buildings attached like barnacles on a wooden jetty post, with spiralling and interconnected pathways connecting them. Many are connected by massive chains spanning vast distances, and sky-carts which allow easy passage between the pillars. 

## History
Originally an ancient seat of power of the Cloud and Storm [[Giant|giants]].
