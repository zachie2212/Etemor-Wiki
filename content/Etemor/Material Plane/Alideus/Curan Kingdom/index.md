## Overview
Currently the largest human civilisation on the [[Etemor/Material Plane/index|Material Plane]]. Mostly made up of smaller towns and villages, with a few large hubs of power and trade, such as [[Isongau]], the capital.

## Government
The head of state and monarch is [[King Richard William Von Destrian Imbertus Curan II|King Curan II]], however most of the day-to-day of the Kingdom's government is managed by the [[Isongau Council]]
