## Overview
The Tavern previously owned by [[Silas Silvertongue]] in [[Brittlefront]]. After a climactic battle between the local authorities and [[The Dust Skulls]] gang, as well as [[Silas Silvertongue|Silas]] and his son [[Cassian Silvertongue]], it has burned down.
