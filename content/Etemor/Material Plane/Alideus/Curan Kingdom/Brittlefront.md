## Overview
Small mining town in the southern region of the [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/index|Curan Kingdom]]. The hometown of [[Etemor/Player Characters/Silas Silvertongue|Silas Silvertongue]].

## Description
A dusty and rough around the edges town, most of the buildings are made of timber, with stone and mortar added later to reinforce. 

## History
Originally, **Brittlefront** was merely an outpost for the mining company known as the [[Etemor/Organisations/Southstone Resource Corporation|Southstone Resource Corporation]] with small and temporary wooden huts for the workers. However, as the value of the earth was realised, and more miners migrated for work. As such, the town was expanded beyond the existing infrastructure, hence its "cobbled-together" look (excuse the pun).

In the 80 years since its founding, **Brittlefront** has expanded quite a bit, and is now recognised as a town by the kingdom. Like all other towns in the [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/index|Curan Kingdom]], it is policed by the [[City Guard]], however [[Etemor/Organisations/Southstone Resource Corporation|Southstone Resources]] still holds a lot of power in the town.
