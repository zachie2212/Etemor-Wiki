## Overview
This poorly named "brook" is actually the largest river in the East of the [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/index|Curan Kingdom]]. It flows from the [[Harpwall Rise]] to the sea to the East, as well as [[Lake Trefoul]].
