## Overview
The Waboia Morass is a swamp in the [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/index|Curan Kingdom]] approximately halfway between [[Darnathmore]] (to the East) and [[The Brittle Gap]] (to the West). It is directly North of the [[Woodwick Brook]].
