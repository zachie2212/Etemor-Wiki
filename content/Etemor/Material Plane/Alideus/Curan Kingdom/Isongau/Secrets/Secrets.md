## Overview
[[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/Isongau|Isongau]]

## Organisations
### [[The Temple of Renewal]]
Wants: more people to join the order, and more people to resurrect
- offering resurrection for free
- the power of resurrection is not given freely. Those resurrected are unknowingly signing their soul away to the Devil Lord [[Asmodeus]].
### [[The Red Rise]]
Wants: to preserve the natural order of life and death
- suspicious of the Temple of Renewal, and wants to stop it, as "something isn't right".
### [[Bloom House]]
Wants: many people to take the retreat, so that there are more components for their ritual
- the same organisation who experimented on [[Macht]]
- those who take the retreat are imbued with [[Weave Crystals]]
- their plan is to use the subjects as components in a country-wide ritual
### [[The Hand]]
Wants: more control of the city government
- Recently the Spider has been replaced. Currently this is a rumour, and not confirmed. 
- the Hand is gaining more power within the [[City Guard]].
### [[City Guard]]
Wants: to crack down on [[The Hand]], and their recent push for power.
- Hook: 
- [[The Hand]] has been gaining more power within the [[City Guard]] recently, through bribes and blackmail.
- There is a split within the Guards due to this corruption.
###  [[King's Guard]]
Wants: to protect the King and his family
- Has a vested interest in the [[Isongau Council]], as they "represent" the King and his power in the city.
### [[The Crown Athenaeum]]
Wants: the preservation and expansion of knowledge, at any cost
- the official library of The Crown.
- Hook: currently the ancient archives deep in the library have been overrun by animated objects and books, as some of the magic of the literature is infecting the archive.
- they want to know how [[The Temple of Renewal]] has the ability to provide resurrection for so cheap.
### [[Isongau Council]]
Wants: to protect and grow the economy of the city
- made up of elected officials from each district of the city, as well as representatives of powerful organisations in the city and Kingdom.
