# The Golden Alembic

Type: Alchemist  
Shopkeeper: Dimaron Falone (Male Half Elf, Bell Ringer)

## Inventory

- Alchemist's Fire (50 gp): A sticky adhesive compound that ignites when exposed to air.
- Antitoxin (50 gp): Drinking this grants advantage on saving throws against poison for 1 hour.
- Oil Flask (1 sp): A ceramic flask of lamp oil useful for fueling lanterns or fire.
- Flash Powder (15 gp): Throws a blinding flash when ignited.
- Noxious Perfume (5 gp): An unpleasant scent that can mask other odors or distract.
- Acid (vial) (25 gp): A corrosive liquid that deals 2d6 acid damage on contact.
- Alchemist's Supplies (50 gp): A complete set of tools for alchemical work.
- Wound Poultice (5 gp): A herbal paste that stabilizes a dying creature.
- Smoke Bomb (10 gp): Creates a cloud of thick smoke in a 10-foot radius.

## Magic Stock (Party Level 5)

- Potion of Climbing (Common, 58 gp): Grants a climbing speed equal to walking speed for 1 hour.
