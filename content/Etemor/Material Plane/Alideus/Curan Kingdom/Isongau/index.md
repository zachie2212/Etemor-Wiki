## Overview
The capital [[Cities|city]] of the [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/index|Curan Kingdom]]. 

## Description
**The City of Rebirth**
Home to hundreds of lords, nobles, and powerful merchants, as well as the [[Etemor/NPCs/King Richard William Von Destrian Imbertus Curan II|King]] himself. This city isn't very big with trade, due to its inconvenient location. The farmland outside of the city mainly exists to provide for the city and the attached military keep. Although, there is much trade with merchants for food from other lands who are willing to make the difficult journey. 
Its main source of income is tax from the whole empire. Accessed by boat from the mainland, as the dangerous and rocky waters of the [[Maw of Quietus]] prevents access from the sea.
Due to its size and population, the city infrastructure is quite advanced, including sewer systems, magically powered aqueducts, stone paved roads, and even public transport systems. The city is brimming with life everywhere.
The city has four main districts:
- **The Docks**. The eastern semi-circle, the poorer district, filled with both trade, work-yards for resources brought from the mainland, and common markets.
- **The Deus**. The western inner-circle, surrounding [[Redmont Citadel]], the castle home of the King, and filled with sites of high society and the great temples of the city. Currently very busy due to the influx of patrons of [[The Temple of Renewal]].
- **The Dowry**. A central hub of trade, most merchants are found here, and there are many places for temporary markets.
- **The Disc**. The circle surrounding The Deus and The Dowry, primarily middle-class residential. 

## History
Once a powerful city in the [[Ali'deum Empire|Ali'deum Empire]], **Isongau** is filled with remnants of the fallen civilisation. Most notably, the gigantic, 100 meter stone statues found throughout the city, which are believed to have once been magically animated constructs created to defend the city. However now they are just overgrown landmarks repurposed as high locations for housing and businesses.
