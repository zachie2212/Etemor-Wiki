## Overview
The son of [[Etemor/Player Characters/Silas Silvertongue|Silas Silvertongue]], he comes from the mining town of [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/Brittlefront|Brittlefront]]. 
Raised around criminals, due to his father's occupation at the [[The Brass Tooth Inn]], **Cassian** fell in with [[The Dust Skulls]] gang. Much like his father, **Cassian** is a natural manipulator, and has used these skills to take control of the gang. 

After discovering his Silas' association with law enforcement, he disowned his father, and they have not seen each other since.
