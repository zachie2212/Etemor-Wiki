## Overview
**Pronouns:** he/him
Orc Devotion Paladin

**Looks:** A tall, gruff looking Orc wearing white and silver armour of a knight of [[Kivalon]], however the crest of the [[House Kivalon]] has been removed.

**Voice:** Stern and powerful.

**Description:** Raised poor, Dura ascended to become a knight of [[Kivalon]] before the kingdom's downfall. He seeks to defend the innocent and bring down tyrants such as the [[House Kivalon]], and their superseder, the treasonous [[House Fidel]].
