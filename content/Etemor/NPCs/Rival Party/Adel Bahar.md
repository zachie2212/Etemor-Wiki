## Overview
**Pronouns:** he/him
Tiefling Abberant Sorcerer

**Looks:** A pale skinned, bald headed tiefling man, he holds himself in an unsettling way, and always looks like their eyes are in a distant place. 

**Voice:** Hollow and distant, their sentences always drift off unfinished.

**Description:** His body is covered in gems embedded in his skin, with a few tattoos on his ribcage. They also have a short code tattooed on their wrist (much like [[Macht]]. Their memory of the past few years is hazy, however they know they escaped captivity in [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/North Alideus/index|North Alideus]].
