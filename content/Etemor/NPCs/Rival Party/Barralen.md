## Overview
**Pronouns:** he/him
Tiefling Champion Fighter

**Looks:** Tall, deep red skin, wide curling horns, and a scar straight down the middle of their vacant right  eye hole. He is very intimidating.

**Voice:** Deep, but kind. Gives the impression of a gentle giant.

**Description:** He comes from a family descended from a powerful warring clan of Devils. He has trained to be a master combatant, and is savage but wise in his battle tactics.
