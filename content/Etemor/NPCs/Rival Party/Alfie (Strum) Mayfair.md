## Overview
**Pronouns:** he/him
Human Fiend Warlock

**Looks:** Strum, as friends call him, is a human man who exudes charm and slyness. Not too tall but very handsome with blonde hair.

**Voice:** Loud and gentlemanly, always sounds like he is smiling.

**Description:** He is a proficient musician who made a deal with an unknown fiendish power to obtain magic to enhance his talent. However, he aims to use his powers of performance and persuasion to steal from the rich to give to the poor. Currently the best way to achieve that is to work with [[The Wanderers]].
