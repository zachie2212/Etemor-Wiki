## Overview
The world of **Etemor**. The name for the entire universe, and location of all [[Plane|Planes]]!

Note that **Etemor** might refer to the entire "universe", i.e. the set of all Planes, or it could also refer specifically to the [[Material Plane]], where most of the story takes place.
