## Overview
An order of priests of [[Lathander]], located in [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/Isongau/index|Isongau]].
In the last year or so, the temple has begun providing resurrections to everyone, free of charge. This has caused a surge of religious fervour, and a cultural shift in the city.
