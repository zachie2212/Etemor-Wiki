## Overview
An old and large mining company primarily operating in the [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/index|Curan Kingdom]] and greater [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/index|Alideus]].

## History
In the aftermath of the [[General/Cataclysm|Cataclysm]], **Southstone Resources** emerged as surviving humans salvaged valuables from the ruins of the fallen [[General/Ali'deum Empire|Ali'deum Empire]]. As time went on, and most of the treasure from the ruins had been recovered (or was to dangerous to retrieve), the **Corporation** pivoted to other mining operations (gemstones, copper, iron, etc.), and is now one of the most powerful companies in [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/index|Alideus]].
