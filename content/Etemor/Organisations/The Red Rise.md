## Overview
A temple of [[The Raven Queen]] in [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/Isongau/index|Isongau]]. A small order in the city, due to the relative unpopularity of the deity. The temple is a humble and dark stone building close to the edge of the The Disc and The Deus.
