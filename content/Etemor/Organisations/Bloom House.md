## Overview
A wellness retreat in [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/Isongau/index|Isongau]], which offers to "renew your personhood", "free yourself", and "connect with the power in all things". 

Those who take the retreat leave as new people, quite literally. They have no memories of their life before the retreat, and many discover they now have a small amount of magical powers. Those who take this retreat are well aware of this, and do so willingly. They seek a new life and freedom from the troubles of their current life.
