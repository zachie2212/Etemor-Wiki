## Overview
[[Etemor/Player Characters/Fret Tango|Fret Tango]]

Written By Stefan

He was a delivery cart man, he had a simple life, delivering goods, cargo, live stock. You name it. For a chance at a bit more coin, he began dealing with a less favourable crowd, crooks and crims. Slowly but surely his cargo was slightly more mysterious, a bit more... hush hush lets say. Said criminals, would transport, (Elixirs, banned potions, illegal halflings) he began thinking, maybe there's more to this mundane life then just a horse and cart...….. 

After months and months of his stint as a low tier criminal Fret began bringing home serious coin. The cargo got deadlier and his pockets got heavier. His lady, [[Janelle]], didn't approve, and like dust in the wind she hit the road. Leaving Fret for a safer path in life. 

But all the glitters are not gold, without Janelle, Fret felt a little empty (he wasn't like super super cut but he was a bit cut, enough cut to pick up the blues).

On one hot summers day, delivering rattling demijohns of some evil red spooky gas liquid, his cart stopped at a cross roads, it was here where a spirit was felt. With no water, a mirage in the distance, Fret sat under a tree at the cross roads when he heard whispers... 

Long story short cunt sold his soul to the devil. He may not have met the actual like devil, he might of been contacted by the spirits in the glass bottles in the cart but yeah he got some really good guitar skills from it. (still don't know what the full catch is, like how that limits him physical without a soul. like what not having a soul means I don't know, but down for whatever).

His recollection is hazy, but when Fret landed at the drop off point, the demijohns (big glass wine container things) were empty, the boss was mad, he lost the job and loss that guy a lot of money. That's guys name was [[Pizar Peroleches]]. 

Now with his new skills he went on down to the tavern for open mic night. (as you do) and he was just great. after playing all the local taverns for some cash, he kept traveling and traveling, as he couldn't stay in one place for too long now, he would see glimpses of "the devil" evil spirits, idk. he was paranoid maybe. He couldn't settle, and from that he became the travelling man he was learning his warlock craft along the way with his new skills.
