## Overview
Written by Seb.

**[[Etemor/Player Characters/Macht|Mac's]]** memories don't start from the facility, the begin being kicked in the gut in an alley as he is hassled to move on. This begins a cycle for Mac. Fall asleep, kick to the gut to wake up. He started judging the kick, bit higher, good form, sharp toe.

He wears raggedy clothes, less people stare that way. The days would be mostly the same begging on a corner. Some days, they'd be a lookout for a robbery or two. But eventually the towns would move him on, or he'd get chased out by the law. Some nights dreams, or more like nightmares. The surge of lightning through him, of scientists watching him with piercing eyes. Others dying in his arms. He knew they were real, but he tried to push them down.

On the road Mac would try to harness his power he wields in his dreams. He takes of his mud-covered hood, revealing the various crystals infused into their skin. One on the top of each of their hands, one at the base of his neck, one just below his ribs, another on his stomach. Four on his spine and two on his shoulders. Each time he would only produce a fraction of what he thought he was capable of.
