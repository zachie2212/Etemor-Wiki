## Overview
There are many different **Planes** in the World of [[Etemor/index|Etemor]]. Each is spatially, and sometimes temporally, separate, and one cannot travel between them without powerful [[magic]] (e.g. the Plane Shift Spell).

### [[Etemor/Material Plane/index|Material Plane]]
