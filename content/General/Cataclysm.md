## Overview
The [[Etemor/Material Plane/index|Material Plane]] of [[Etemor/index|Etemor]] was once covered with great and magically gifted civilisations. What happened to them?
While much of the **Cataclysm** is unknown, it is believed that the hubris of ancient peoples led to the [[General/Gods|gods]] smiting and destroying their civilisations in a great war. 

Many mortals fought on the side of their god, while others fought against them. The result was the destruction of all powerful civilisations on the [[Etemor/Material Plane/index|Material Plane]], the death of many gods, and the creation of the [[General/Divine Gate|Divine Gate]], to separate the gods from the mortals, to protect themselves, and their creation.
