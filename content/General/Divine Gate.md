## Overview
The **Divine Gate** is a powerful magical force which separates the [[Inner Planes]] (i.e. the [[Etemor/Material Plane/index|Material Plane]], the [[Elemental Planes]], the [[Feywild]], and the [[Shadowfell]]) from the [[Outer Planes]].

It was created by the [[gods]] after the [[Cataclysm]] to protect themselves from mortals, and to protect the mortals from their power.

The creation of the **Divine Gate** fundamentally changed the gods. 
Firstly, by restricting themselves to only exist within their domains, the gods each grew in power, but lessened their reach. As such, they cannot be killed (or it is MUCH harder that before the Cataclysm), but they have less direct contact with their followers.
Consequently, present day worship in [[Etemor/index|Etemor]] has changed, and mortals view the gods differently, as they are less of an active force in their lives like they had been historically.
