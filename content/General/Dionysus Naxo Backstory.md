## Overview
Written by Ross.

In the great castle in the kingdom of [[Monolithios]], Queen #todo would give birth to the first prince of the heavenly plain, [[Etemor/Player Characters/Dionysus Naxo|Dionysus Naxo]]. Despite being of royal blood and heir to the throne, Dion was raised the same way as any [[Monolithian]] boy. He learned the ways of their people and learned to fight at a young age. His proficiency with the sword granted him the rank of general, a role he would go on to excel in the war between the heavens and the hells.

This war has been waged for centuries ever since the [[Cataclysm]]. It was a war of ideologies and conflicting beliefs, each side believing the other should be eradicated by any means. Dion was taught to despise the demons of the hellish realm, believing they were the root of all evil in not only the astral plain, but the mortal plain.
  
Dion served valiantly during the war, leading the charge on many battles and even leading the [[Monolithians]] to victory on several occasions - he was praised highly and earned his place as not only a great warrior, but a worthy king. 

He earned a promotion to lead a small team of soldiers to infiltrate the hell kingdom to gather intelligence, as the [[Monolithians]] believed the demons were planning to invade and conquer the mortal plain. This mission however, saw the death of his team and and [[Etemor/Player Characters/Dionysus Naxo|Dion's]] capture, where he was held in the demons dungeons. It was here were he met the demon princess named [[Estus]]. The two formed a bond - [[Estus]] would sneak into the castles dungeons late at night to spend time with Dion. 

Their bond grew stronger and [[Estus]] believed she could convince her father to set Dion free and potentially bring peace between the warring factions. However, the [[Monolithians]] had learned of the capture of Dion and his team and planned a full frontal assault on the demon castle. They breached the castles defences late at night as Dion and [[Estus]] were having their nightly rendezvous. In the chaos, [[Estus]] set Dion free and the both planned to escape that night. Dion with nothing but his sword in one hand and lover in the other attempted to flee the castle where they encountered the invading [[Monolithians]]. In an instant, an arrow was fired into the heart of [[Estus]]. Dion cried out, the soldiers watching in shock seeing their soon to be king grieving the loss of the enemy. Dion held [[Estus]] in his arms as she whispered her dying breath, a demonic spell transferring her life energy into the closest object - his sword.

The [[Monolithians]] extracted with Dion as they started to become overwhelmed by demonic forces. While King #todo was relieved to have his son back, his joy turned to horror when he learned the truth of [[Etemor/Player Characters/Dionysus Naxo|Dion's]] time in demon captivity. Dion was left with nothing - the love of his life was taken and he lost the respect of his own people. He carried himself to the top of the Monolith - the highest point in the kingdom carrying his sword with him. Believing he had nothing else to live for, he looked upon the land as he pierced the sword through his chest, falling off the mountain. 

[[Etemor/Player Characters/Dionysus Naxo|Dion]], hearing whispers of his fallen lover, awoke. A wound now carved into his chest he sat in a field of grass, his sword laying beside him. He no longer had his wings and the powers granted to him through his angelic form seemed to have been stripped from him. He found himself in the realm of mortals and seemingly, being transformed into a mortal himself. 

[[Etemor/Player Characters/Dionysus Naxo|Dion]] wanders for many months, living out his days as a mortal. Though he had been given a second chance at life, the pain of his loss and now the abandonment of his home left him with deep sorrow. He would have nightmares, believing [[Estus]] was speaking to him - even hearing her voice in his waking hours. Dion sought to silence these voices through a discovery he made upon his first visit to [[The Brass Tooth Inn]]. He turned to the drink to quell his spiralling mind - and became a regular at the Inn. He kept to himself, not speaking to anyone - not even the quick-witted [[Etemor/Player Characters/Silas Silvertongue|Silas Silvertongue]] could tempt the fallen angel to utter a word. 

Dion carried on this way for months, but it seemed the more he drank the louder the voice of [[Estus]] got. One night it became too much to bare and Dion rushed out of the Inn in a rage. He sprinted off into the night - rage and emotion spiralling. That is until he stumbled upon a creature. Without hesitation Dion drew his sword and battled the beast - and upon slaying the creature the voice of [[Estus]] was no longer a whisper. Her voice was clearer than ever - looking down at his sword the hilt had become wrapped in a deep, blood red colour. That was when he realised - he was not being haunted by the ghost of his lover, she was beside him the whole time. 

Her words rang clear - a purpose. She asked that he go out seeking the truth of not only his people, but hers as well and that learning that truth would grant him the ultimate power that could finally bring peace to his realm and bring her back.
