## Overview
A powerful mineral which can enhance and power magic. Can be used to cover the material component cost of any spell.
Similar in feel to fibreglass or asbestos, as it is composed of many tiny strands of needle-like thread, which embed themselves in the skin of anyone who touches it.
This substance is highly sought after by spellcasters due to its usefulness.

Nobody knows how this material can be produced, however it is naturally found in the earth of highly magical areas, such as some battlefields from the [[General/Cataclysm|Cataclysm]], and also in the ruins of the [[General/Ali'deum Empire|Ali'deum Empire]].
It is believed that mages living during the [[General/Ali'deum Empire|Ali'deum Empire]] were able to refine [[The Weave]] to create this substance, hence its name.
