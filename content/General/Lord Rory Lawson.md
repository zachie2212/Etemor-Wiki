## Overview
Lord of the area surrounding [[Etemor/Material Plane/Alideus/Curan Kingdom/Darnathmore|Darnathmore]].
Only came into power in the last year, after the death of his father, the late [[Lord Cameron Lawson]].

## Personality
Since he is quite a young lord, he is trying to come across as very serious and hard. 

## Motivations / Wants
Since he wants to be taken seriously, he is cracking down on crime in his land, coming into conflict with [[Etemor/Organisations/The Hand|The Hand]], and other local and [[Vassal Houses|Vassal]] lords as a result. Some think he is taking a very naïve approach to this endeavour.
