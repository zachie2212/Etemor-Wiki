## Overview
**Magic** is the ability possessed by some individuals to manipulate the ambient energies of the world to produce desired results. 
In addition to granting spellcasting abilities, **magic** is an influential force in the Realms. It serves as the source of energy for enchanted items, allows some to travel across the world, and through the extra-planar cosmology and even shaped the physical landscape of the planet.
